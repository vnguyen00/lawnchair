Introducing Minimal Mode!

Minimal Mode gives you a second, distraction-free home screen with only the apps you choose. Unlike graying out apps in Digital Wellbeing, it switches your whole home screen to a separate, simpler one. When you turn it off, your normal home screen comes back exactly as you left it.

To set it up, open Lawnchair Settings, tap **Minimal Mode**, pick your **Allowed apps**, then turn on **Use Minimal Mode**.

## What's new

**A separate home screen**
• Minimal Mode has its own home screen layout. The first time, it starts with only your allowed apps; after that, any changes you make to it are kept for next time
• Your normal home screen, folders and widgets are saved and restored when you turn Minimal Mode off
• A small "Minimal Mode" label at the top of the home screen shows it's on. Tap it to open Minimal Mode settings

**Allowed apps first in the app drawer**
• Allowed apps appear at the top of the app drawer, under their own ★ section
• Every other app is grayed out below them, in folders too, and won't open
• Blocked apps are left out of dock suggestions

**Blocks apps opened from anywhere** (optional)
• With Lawnchair's accessibility service on, blocked apps are also stopped when opened from notifications, links, recents or other apps. You're sent back home with a short message
• The phone, keyboard, system UI, and permission and install prompts always keep working
• Minimal Mode settings show whether this is on, with a shortcut to turn it on

**Lock it so you stick with it**
• Choose what's needed to turn Minimal Mode off or change its settings: nothing, a Minimal Mode PIN (which someone else can keep), or your phone's screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password)
• Once unlocked, settings stay open for 5 minutes. Turning Minimal Mode on locks it again

**Schedule**
• Turn Minimal Mode on and off automatically at set times on chosen days, for example weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00
• Schedules that run past midnight work, and a start or end missed while the phone was off is applied when it turns back on
• Turning Minimal Mode off by hand during a scheduled time keeps it off until the next start

**Quick Settings tile**
• Add the Minimal Mode tile to turn it on with one tap from anywhere. When a lock is set, tapping the tile while Minimal Mode is on opens settings to unlock

## Good to know

• **Turning on app blocking for apps you sideloaded:** on Android 13 and later, the accessibility switch may be grayed out at first. Open Lawnchair's App info, tap ⋮, and choose **Allow restricted settings**, then try again
• **Forgotten PIN:** the only way to remove it is clearing Lawnchair's data, which also resets your home screen. A schedule's end time still turns Minimal Mode off
• **Schedule timing:** a start or end can come up to about 10 minutes late, or later if your phone is idle. It takes effect right away when you go to the home screen
• **Lawndeck:** Minimal Mode can't be used with the Lawndeck home layout, since both replace the home screen
• **Not covered yet:** apps you allow after first using Minimal Mode aren't added to its home screen automatically (find them in the app drawer), and apps in split screen or picture-in-picture aren't blocked
• **Not a parental control:** booting in safe mode, using adb, or changing the default home app in Settings with blocking off can still get around Minimal Mode
